## Spoty is in development

There are no Spoty downloads or published releases yet. The project is being
rebuilt from the Spotifast codebase, and its packaging and release process
will be prepared as the Spoty app takes shape.

Follow [Spoty on GitHub](https://github.com/efeio/spoty) for development
updates. When the first Spoty release is ready, its installers and checksums
will be published on the
[GitHub releases page](https://github.com/efeio/spoty/releases).
