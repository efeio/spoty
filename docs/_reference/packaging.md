---
title: Packaging status
description: Spoty's current packaging and release status.
nav_order: 20
---

Spoty does not have published installers, package-store listings, or an update
feed yet. The existing packaging scripts came from the Spotifast starting
codebase and are being adapted to Spoty's app identity and release process.

Follow the [Spoty repository](https://github.com/efeio/spoty) for release
announcements. Until the first Spoty release is published, instructions for
installing Spotifast do not apply to Spoty.
