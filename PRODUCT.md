# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who listen to Spotify on desktop, from listeners who want to get to
their music quickly to listeners who organize and explore a large library.
They use Spoty while working, relaxing, or actively choosing what to hear.

## Product Purpose

Spoty is a native desktop Spotify client that makes finding, organizing, and
playing Spotify music feel straightforward and considered. Success means a
listener can get to the right music quickly, understand what is playing, and
discover more without the interface competing with the music.

## Brand Personality

Calm, refined, and trustworthy. The experience should feel musical and
personal, with careful visual hierarchy and dependable controls.

## Anti-references

- Do not reproduce Spotify's distinctive interface or branding.
- Avoid crowded screens, gratuitous animation, and decoration that distracts
  from choosing and listening to music.

## Design Principles

1. Keep listening and music selection at the center of the experience.
2. Make the next useful action easy to find without filling the screen with
   controls.
3. Let artwork and editorial hierarchy make music discovery feel personal.
4. Keep playback state and controls immediate, clear, and dependable.
5. Preserve the speed and keyboard access expected of a native desktop app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accommodation has been requested yet. Preserve keyboard
navigation, readable contrast in both themes, clear non-color state cues, and
restrained motion as the interface evolves.
